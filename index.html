Leadership was inconsistent across every team. The organisation was scaling fast with no shared resource, no documented procedures, and no common language — every procedural question came back to People & Culture reactively.

The gap

No centralised guidance for managers. New team leads onboarding at pace with nothing to reference.

The opportunity

Build a practical, research-backed resource that reflected how leadership actually worked in a fast-moving startup.

⭐ Effective Leadership — where all three domains intersect: Delegation · Collaboration · Stakeholder Management

Agile Leadership Model

Validated the dominant leadership style (55.6%) already in practice — the toolkit was built to strengthen it, not replace it with rigid process.

SBI Feedback Model

Situation–Behaviour–Impact framework embedded in the Interpersonal domain for consistent, fair, emotionally neutral feedback delivery.

SMART Goals

Embedded in Team Management to help leaders set clear expectations, track progress and conduct meaningful check-in conversations.

Competency Radar Analysis

Translated survey data into a visual that identified where strengths clustered and where development support was needed most.

Checklists & procedures

04

Onboarding checklist

7 items
0 of 7 completed
Added to department-specific onboarding Slack channel
Attended kickoff meeting (held every other Monday)
New intern invited to team Slack group channel
One-on-one meeting scheduled to clarify expectations
Added to relevant team workspaces (Notion, Google Drive)
New Intern Employee Form completed
Introduction made at All-Hands meeting

Offboarding checklist

8 items
0 of 8 completed
Intern notified of upcoming end of term (14-day notice)
Decision to offboard or extend confirmed
Intern Status Change Approval Form submitted
Slack access removed by P&C team
Removed from Team Workspaces in Notion
Google Drive access revoked
Outstanding tasks reassigned to remaining team members
Exit questionnaire sent to departing intern

Workforce planning checklist

8 items
0 of 8 completed
Current team headcount reviewed
Task timelines estimated (average hours per deliverable)
Team workloads evaluated against project demands
Performance benchmarks set using historical data
Future headcount needs estimated based on project scope
Workforce Planning Sheet updated and submitted to P&C
Priority recruitment positions clearly flagged
Job descriptions reviewed and updated with P&C team

Impact & skills

05

✅ What changed

Managers self-served routine HR queries independently for the first time
New team leads onboarded with a structured resource from day one
Research phase created cross-team reflection that hadn't existed before

🔁 What I'd do differently

Build in a 60-day post-launch survey to measure manager confidence
Close the feedback loop the research phase opened
Embed impact measurement into the project scope from the start
